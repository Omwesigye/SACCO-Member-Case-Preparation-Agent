# The Tier 4 Microfinance Institutions and Money Lenders (SACCO) Regulations, 2020

**Statutory Instrument 2020 No. 81**  
**Gazetted Date:** 15th June, 2020 | **Commencement Date:** 1st July, 2020  
**Authority:** Enacted under Section 112 of the Tier 4 Microfinance Institutions and Money Lenders Act, 2016 by the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development in consultation with UMRA.

---

## Regulation 10: Credit Policy and Lending Operations
1. A SACCO shall formulate, adopt, and implement a written Credit Policy approved by its Board of Directors in compliance with these Regulations.
2. A SACCO shall not disburse any loan facility without:
   * (a) A formal written loan application signed by the member.
   * (b) Documented verification of the borrower’s identity, physical residence, and credit history.
   * (c) A written assessment of the borrower's repayment capacity and cash flows.
   * (d) Adequate security or legally binding guarantorship documentation.

## Regulation 12: Credit Exposure and Concentration Limits
1. **Single Borrower Exposure Cap:** A SACCO shall not grant a loan or credit facility to an individual borrower where the aggregate outstanding credit exposure exceeds **five percent (5%) of the SACCO’s Core Capital**.
2. **Connected Borrowers Cap:** A SACCO shall not grant credit to a group of interconnected borrowers, family business, or economic entity where the aggregate exposure exceeds **ten percent (10%) of Core Capital**.
3. **Core Capital Defined:** Core capital consists of fully paid-up ordinary member shares, unimpaired statutory reserves, and retained earnings.

## Regulation 13: Insider Lending Restrictions
1. A loan to a Director, member of the Supervisory Committee, Chief Executive Officer, or credit employee of the SACCO:
   * (a) Shall not be granted on terms and conditions (interest rate, tenor, security) more favorable than those offered to ordinary members.
   * (b) Must be reviewed and unanimously approved by the full Board of Directors.
   * (c) The applicant insider must recuse themselves from all deliberations and voting.
2. **Aggregate Insider Limit:** The total outstanding loans to all directors, committee members, and employees shall not in aggregate exceed **twenty-five percent (25%) of the SACCO’s Core Capital**.

## Regulation 14: Mandatory Liquidity Reserve Ratio
1. Every licensed SACCO shall at all times maintain a minimum liquidity reserve of not less than **fifteen percent (15%) of its total member savings deposits and short-term liabilities**.
2. **Qualifying Liquid Assets:** For the purpose of sub-regulation (1), qualifying assets consist exclusively of:
   * (a) Notes and coins held in the SACCO's cash safes/vaults.
   * (b) Unencumbered balances held in demand deposit accounts with licensed commercial banks in Uganda.
   * (c) Treasury Bills and Government Securities issued by the Bank of Uganda maturing within 91 days.
3. If a SACCO’s liquidity reserve falls below 15%, the SACCO shall **immediately suspend all new loan approvals and disbursements** until the 15% ratio is restored.

## Regulation 16: External Borrowing Ceiling
A SACCO shall not incur external debts or borrowings from financial institutions or development partners exceeding **twenty-five percent (25%) of its total assets** without prior written authorization from UMRA.

## Regulation 18 & Schedule 2: Loan Portfolio Classification and Provisioning
Every SACCO shall, at the end of each calendar month, classify its gross loan portfolio and compute specific loan loss provisions according to the statutory criteria in Schedule 2:

| Classification Tier | Arrears Benchmark | Minimum Specific Provision Percentage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Performing (Pass)** | 0 to 30 days | **1.0%** (General collective provision) |
| **Watch (Special Mention)** | 31 to 60 days | **5.0%** |
| **Substandard** | 61 to 90 days | **20.0%** |
| **Doubtful** | 91 to 180 days | **50.0%** |
| **Loss** | Greater than 180 days | **100.0%** (Full impairment) |

*Suspension of Interest:* When a loan reaches Substandard classification ($>60$ days past due), the SACCO must immediately suspend interest accrual in the income statement.
