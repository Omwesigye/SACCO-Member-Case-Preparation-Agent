# UMRA Consumer Protection Guidelines for Tier 4 Microfinance Institutions & SACCOs

**Regulatory Body:** Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority (UMRA)  
**Legal Framework:** Enacted pursuant to Section 112 of the Tier 4 Microfinance Institutions and Money Lenders Act, 2016  
**Applicability:** Mandatory for all licensed SACCOs and Tier 4 microfinance operators in Uganda.

---

## 1. Core Principles of Member and Consumer Protection
Every licensed SACCO must embed the following fundamental consumer rights across all loan origination, savings management, and debt recovery operations:
1. **Fair and Equitable Treatment:** Non-discrimination on grounds of gender, tribe, religion, marital status, or political affiliation.
2. **Transparent and Plain Disclosure:** Clear communication of all product terms, interest calculation formulas, and charges in language understood by the borrower.
3. **Protection of Member Privacy:** Strict adherence to Ugandan data privacy laws; no disclosure of member credit or deposit details to unauthorized third parties.
4. **Reliable and Expeditious Redress:** Accessible, no-cost mechanisms to lodge and resolve grievances.

## 2. Truth in Lending and Full Pricing Transparency
* **2.1 Prohibition of Hidden Fees:** A SACCO shall not levy any charge, ledger administration fee, processing commission, or insurance deduction unless explicitly stated in the Board-approved fee schedule and formally accepted in writing by the member.
* **2.2 Mandatory Key Facts Statement (KFS):**
  Prior to executing a loan agreement, the SACCO must provide the member with an individual, itemized **Key Facts Statement** highlighting:
  * Approved loan principal and net disbursed cash amount.
  * Total interest payable over the loan life.
  * Total non-interest fees and statutory taxes (Total Cost of Credit).
  * Effective Annual Percentage Rate (APR), computed using reducing balance methodology.
  * Late payment penalty structure.
* **2.3 Deceptive Advertising:** Quoting monthly flat interest rates without explicitly disclosing the annual reducing-balance equivalent is prohibited.

## 3. Fair Debt Recovery and Debt Collection Code of Conduct
All credit staff, recovery task forces, and external debt collection agencies acting on behalf of a SACCO must strictly adhere to the following rules:
* **3.1 Permissible Contact Windows:** Contact by phone, SMS, email, or physical visit is restricted strictly to **8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Saturday**. No collection visits on Sundays or public holidays.
* **3.2 Prohibition of Harassment and Public Shaming:**
  * No visiting a borrower's employer, office, or place of worship to reveal delinquency or embarrass the borrower.
  * No displaying default lists in public markets or cooperative notice boards accessible to non-members.
  * No threats of bodily harm, criminal prosecution for ordinary contractual debt, or extra-judicial seizure.
* **3.3 Exempt Household Necessities:**
  Under no circumstances may a recovery officer or bailiff seize:
  * Children's educational books, uniforms, or school equipment.
  * Essential personal clothing, bedding, and mattresses.
  * Primary cooking utensils, food reserves, and clean water containers necessary for family sustenance.
* **3.4 Mandatory Pre-Auction Notices:** Pledged immovable or registered movable collateral cannot be advertised for public auction without serving written 30-day and 14-day statutory notices of default and obtaining a certified professional valuation report within the last 6 months.

## 4. Dispute Resolution and Complaints Handling Standards
* **4.1 Free Complaints Mechanism:** Every branch must offer a simple, cost-free mechanism for members to lodge complaints verbally or in writing.
* **4.2 Statutory Timeframes:**
  * Written acknowledgement within **48 hours**.
  * Final investigation, substantive resolution, and written explanation within **fourteen (14) business days**.
* **4.3 Complaints Register:** SACCOs must maintain an immutable Complaints Register inspected by UMRA during annual supervisory visits.

## 5. Regulatory Escalation to UMRA
Where a SACCO fails to resolve a dispute within thirty (30) days or where a member alleges predatory lending or harassment:
* The member has the statutory right to petition UMRA directly.
* UMRA may summon the SACCO Chief Executive Officer and Credit Committee to a formal conciliation hearing.
* UMRA may order immediate restitution, refund of unlawful fees, or suspension of unauthorized recovery actions.
