# Credit Policy and Lending Procedures Manual

## 1. Objectives and Scope of the Policy
* **1.1 Purpose:** This Manual establishes sound, transparent, and legally compliant lending standards for the SACCO in accordance with the Tier 4 Microfinance Institutions and Money Lenders Act, 2016 and UMRA SACCO Regulations 2020.
* **1.2 Scope:** Applies to all loan products, credit staff, loan officers, management, and the Credit Committee involved in credit origination, appraisal, approval, disbursement, and monitoring.

## 2. Core Credit Principles (The 5 C’s of Credit)
Every credit appraisal must document evidence against:
1. **Character:** Integrity, cooperative standing, past repayment history, credit bureau/internal inquiry results, and community repute.
2. **Capacity:** Proven cash flow, verified disposable income, business viability, and debt-servicing ability.
3. **Capital:** Member's equity in the SACCO (shares and unencumbered savings balance).
4. **Collateral:** Pledged savings, verifiable physical assets, titled land, registered chattels, or qualified personal guarantors.
5. **Conditions:** Purpose of the facility, economic feasibility, seasonal/market trends, and borrower vulnerability.

## 3. General Borrower Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for any loan facility, an applicant must satisfy all of the following conditions:
* **3.1 Membership Duration:** Must have been an active, registered member for a minimum of six (6) consecutive calendar months prior to the date of application.
* **3.2 Minimum Shareholding:** Must have fully paid up the statutory minimum of twenty (20) ordinary shares (UGX 200,000).
* **3.3 Regular Savings Track Record:** Must show consistent monthly savings deposits into their compulsory/voluntary savings account for at least six (6) consecutive months without interruption.
* **3.4 Clean Delinquency Record:** Must have no active non-performing, rescheduled, or defaulted loan within the SACCO, and must not have defaulted on any SACCO loan in the preceding twelve (12) months.
* **3.5 Age and Legal Capacity:** Must be at least 18 years of age and legally competent to enter into enforceable contracts under Ugandan law.
* **3.6 KYC Compliance:** Full Level-2 KYC verification is mandatory (National ID / NIN verified via NIRA or visual inspection, verified physical residential address, LC1 letter, and certified passport photograph).

## 4. Credit Limits and Exposure Multipliers
* **4.1 Savings Multiplier:** The maximum loan facility allowable to any single member shall not exceed **three (3) times** the member’s unencumbered savings balance for non-agricultural facilities, or up to **four (4) times** for secured agricultural/development facilities.
* **4.2 Single Borrower Exposure Limit:** Pursuant to UMRA SACCO Regulations 2020 Regulation 12:
  * No individual borrower shall receive loans exceeding **5%** of the SACCO’s Core Capital.
  * No group or connected borrowers shall receive aggregate credit exceeding **10%** of the SACCO’s Core Capital.
* **4.3 Absolute Ceilings by Product:** Maximum borrowing caps are defined in the approved [Loan Product Catalogue](file:///d:/Sacco-Agent/RAG/04_loan_product_catalogue.md).

## 5. Repayment Capacity and Debt Service Ratio (DSR)
* **5.1 Affordability Rule:** Total monthly debt obligations (including the proposed new SACCO loan installment plus all other proven debt obligations) must **not exceed 50% of the applicant’s verifiable net monthly income** (or net operating cash flow for business applicants).
  $$\text{Debt Service Ratio (DSR)} = \frac{\text{Total Monthly Loan Repayments}}{\text{Verifiable Net Monthly Income}} \times 100\% \le 50\%$$
* **5.2 Minimum Take-Home Pay:** For salaried members, the remaining net monthly salary after all deductions must not be less than 33% of gross salary or the national living wage threshold.
* **5.3 Income Verification Standards:**
  * Salaried employees: Last 3 months certified bank statements and latest 3 consecutive certified pay slips.
  * Business / Informal traders: Minimum 6 months SACCO / Bank statements, verified sales cash book, physical business inspection report by the Loan Officer, and inventory valuation.
  * Agricultural producers: Farm visit inspection report, harvest history records, off-taker agreements, and crop cycle cash-flow calendar.

## 6. Security and Guarantor Requirements
* **6.1 Security Coverage:** Every loan facility must be secured to a minimum of **100% of the loan principal plus anticipated interest**.
* **6.2 Security Components:** Security may consist of:
  1. Pledged member savings (cash collateral). Pledged savings cannot be withdrawn until the loan is paid in full.
  2. Qualified member personal guarantors (jointly and severally liable).
  3. Movable assets registered under the Security Interest in Movable Property Act (SIMPO), accompanied by an official valuation report.
  4. Registered land titles or verified customary land agreements backed by local council confirmation.
* **6.3 Guarantor Eligibility Rules:**
  * A guarantor must be an active SACCO member for at least six (6) months.
  * A member cannot guarantee loans totaling more than their own free (unencumbered) savings.
  * A borrower cannot self-guarantee.
  * A member who is in loan arrears or currently guaranteeing a defaulted loan is disqualified from acting as a guarantor.
  * Spousal consent is mandatory where family residential land or primary household assets are pledged.

## 7. Interest Rate Structure and Calculation Methodology
* **7.1 Calculation Method:** In strict adherence to UMRA transparency standards, all term loans shall be calculated on a **Reducing Balance (Amortized)** method. The Flat rate method is prohibited for term facilities.
* **7.2 Benchmark Rates:** Current lending rates are set forth in the [Board-Approved Interest Rate and Fee Schedule](file:///d:/Sacco-Agent/RAG/20_board_approved_interest_rate_and_fee_schedule.md).
* **7.3 Monthly Amortization Formula:**
  $$\text{PMT} = P \times \frac{r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}$$
  Where:
  * $P$ = Loan Principal
  * $r$ = Periodic Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate / 12)
  * $n$ = Total number of monthly installments

## 8. Delegation of Loan Approval Authority
No single individual may originate, appraise, and approve a loan independently. Strict segregation of duties applies:
* **Tier 1 (Branch Credit Officer / Senior Loan Officer):** Appraisal and recommendation only (No independent approval).
* **Tier 2 (Branch Manager):** Approvals up to **UGX 3,000,000** for Emergency and School Fees facilities meeting 100% savings backing.
* **Tier 3 (Credit Committee):** Approvals between **UGX 3,000,001 and UGX 20,000,000**.
* **Tier 4 (Full Board of Directors):** Approvals above **UGX 20,000,000**, all policy exceptions, and all insider loans (loans to staff, committee members, or directors).

## 9. Loan Case Preparation Protocol for Loan Officers
Before submitting any loan file to the Credit Committee, the Loan Officer must assemble a verified **Loan Case Pack** comprising:
1. Completed, signed Loan Application Form with signed Member Consent.
2. Verified Member KYC Dossier (NIN check, passport photo, LC1 letter).
3. Member financial statement (Savings ledger, share balance, loan repayment track record).
4. Completed Loan Appraisal Checklist showing DSR calculation and verification notes.
5. Signed Guarantor vetting forms with verified savings balances.
6. Asset inspection / collateral verification report (where applicable).
7. Illustrative Repayment Schedule generated by the deterministic system calculator.
8. Written credit officer assessment of risks, mitigants, and formal recommendation.

## 10. Delinquency Management, Recovery, and Grace Periods
* **10.1 Grace Periods:** A grace period of not more than one (1) repayment cycle (30 days) may be granted for agricultural production loans where harvest cycles dictate. No grace period applies to commercial or emergency loans.
* **10.2 Arrears Reminders:**
  * Day 1 to 7 past due: SMS and phone call courtesy reminder.
  * Day 8 to 30 past due: Formal written reminder and physical visit.
  * Day 31+ past due: Escalation to Guarantors, classification as Watch/Substandard, and freeze on guarantor savings.
