# Tier 4 Regulatory Guidance for SACCOs

**Issuing Regulatory Body:** Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority (UMRA)  
**Governing Statute:** The Tier 4 Microfinance Institutions and Money Lenders Act, 2016 (Act No. 18 of 2016)  
**Statutory Purpose:** To prescribe licensing benchmarks, prudential operating ratios, fit-and-proper leadership criteria, and supervisory reporting for Savings and Credit Cooperative Societies (SACCOs) in Uganda.

---

## 1. Regulatory Authority and Mandate
* **1.1 Establishment:** The Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority is established under Section 6 of the Tier 4 Act, 2016 to license, regulate, and supervise Tier 4 microfinance institutions comprising SACCOs, non-deposit-taking microfinance institutions, moneylenders, and community self-help groups.
* **1.2 Primacy of Law:** All internal SACCO bylaws, board resolutions, and lending manuals operate subordinate to the Tier 4 Act and UMRA regulations. Any internal clause conflicting with UMRA guidance is null and void to the extent of the inconsistency.

## 2. Licensing Requirements and Capital Adequacy
* **2.1 Licensing Pre-Conditions:**
  To obtain and retain an operating license from UMRA, a SACCO must:
  1. Hold a Certificate of Registration from the Registrar of Cooperative Societies under the Cooperative Societies Act, Cap 112.
  2. Maintain a minimum of thirty (30) fully registered, active members sharing an identifiable common bond.
  3. Hold unimpaired **Minimum Core Capital of not less than UGX 50,000,000** (or higher threshold as determined for large urban/transacting SACCOs).
  4. Maintain an institutional capital ratio of at least 10% of total assets.
* **2.2 Compulsory Statutory Reserve:** Every SACCO must allocate not less than twenty percent (20%) of its annual audited net operational surplus into a non-distributable Statutory Reserve Fund before declaring any member dividend or bonus.

## 3. Fit-and-Proper Criteria for Directors and Key Staff
* **3.1 Statutory Vetting:** Under Section 42 of the Tier 4 Act, no person shall be elected or appointed as a Director, Supervisory Committee member, Chief Executive Officer, or Chief Finance Officer of a SACCO without obtaining UMRA fit-and-proper clearance.
* **3.2 Disqualifying Attributes:**
  * Prior conviction of an offence involving fraud, bribery, forgery, perjury, or money laundering.
  * Personal bankruptcy or having been an executive/director of an institution placed under liquidation or statutory management within the past five (5) years.
  * History of loan default or write-off in any financial institution.

## 4. Prudential Returns and Inspection Standards
* **4.1 Periodic Regulatory Reporting:**
  * **Quarterly Prudential Returns:** SACCOs must submit quarterly financial statements, Portfolio-at-Risk (PAR) aging schedules, liquidity reports, and large-exposure filings within fifteen (15) days of the end of each quarter.
  * **Annual Audited Statements:** Full statutory audit conducted by an authorized external audit firm recognized by the Institute of Certified Public Accountants of Uganda (ICPAU), submitted to UMRA within three (3) months after the close of the financial year.
* **4.2 On-Site Regulatory Inspections:** UMRA inspectors are authorized by law to enter SACCO premises unannounced, inspect loan appraisal registers, review committee minutes, and verify collateral registers.

## 5. Supervisory Interventions and Sanctions
Where a SACCO breaches statutory lending limits, fails to maintain liquidity ratios, or operates recklessly:
1. **Administrative Directives:** Prohibition on paying dividends, freezing of credit expansion, or removal of non-compliant officers.
2. **Statutory Penalties:** Daily compounding monetary fines levied on the SACCO and individual culpable directors.
3. **Statutory Management / License Revocation:** Assumption of institutional management by UMRA to protect member deposits.
