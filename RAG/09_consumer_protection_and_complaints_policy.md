# Consumer Protection, Transparency and Member Complaints Handling Policy

## 1. Purpose and Guiding Philosophy
* **1.1 Purpose:** To operationalize fair treatment, transparency, non-discriminatory access, and ethical lending practices across all member touchpoints, in compliance with UMRA Consumer Protection Guidelines.
* **1.2 Principle of Fairness:** The SACCO treats members as cooperative owners, not mere commercial counterparties. No fee or charge may be levied unless expressly approved by the Board and disclosed prior to contract signing.

## 2. Transparent Pricing and Mandatory Disclosure
* **2.1 Key Facts Statement (KFS):**
  Every approved borrower must be issued a standardized, written **Key Facts Statement** in English and the member's preferred local language (e.g., Runyankore/Luganda) prior to signing the loan contract.
* **2.2 Mandatory Disclosures on the KFS:**
  1. Approved loan amount and net disbursed cash amount.
  2. Nominal monthly interest rate and effective **Annual Percentage Rate (APR)**.
  3. Total Cost of Credit (TCC), detailing every single fee, appraisal levy, insurance premium, and stamp duty.
  4. Complete month-by-month repayment schedule reflecting reducing balance amortization.
  5. Exact penalty charges applicable if payment is delayed.
* **2.3 Cooling-Off Period:**
  Borrowers are granted a statutory **48-hour cooling-off period** following loan agreement signing, during which they may cancel the loan without penalty (provided disbursed funds are refunded intact).

## 3. Ethical Debt Collection and Anti-Harassment Standards
* **3.1 Prohibited Recovery Practices:**
  In accordance with UMRA guidelines, SACCO recovery officers, credit staff, and external bailiffs are strictly prohibited from:
  * Contacting or visiting the borrower or guarantors outside permitted hours (**8:00 AM to 6:00 PM**).
  * Visiting a member’s place of employment or church/mosque to publicly humiliate them.
  * Using abusive, profane, or threatening language.
  * Seizing essential household goods, children's school items, clothing, bedding, or primary cooking utensils.
* **3.2 Collateral Realization Protocol:**
  Seizure and auction of registered pledged collateral can only proceed after serving:
  1. First formal default notice (Day 31).
  2. Final statutory demand notice (Day 60).
  3. 14-day notice of intention to sell registered security.
  All sales must be through a licensed, bonded auctioneer at fair market valuation.

## 4. Complaints Lodging and Redressal Mechanism
* **4.1 Dedicated Channels:** Members may lodge grievances free of charge through:
  * Dedicated Toll-Free Telephone Line: `0800-SACCO-CARE`
  * Physical Complaints Register located at each branch reception desk.
  * Sealed Confidential Complaints Box opened only by the Supervisory Committee.
  * Dedicated Email: `complaints@kazo-sacco.co.ug`.
* **4.2 Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for Resolution:**
  * **Acknowledgement:** Within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt.
  * **Investigation & Feedback:** Written formal response within **fourteen (14) business days**.

## 5. Right of Appeal to the Regulator (UMRA)
* **5.1 Regulatory Recourse:** If a member is dissatisfied with the internal decision of the SACCO Board or if a complaint remains unresolved after thirty (30) days, the member has the unfettered statutory right to petition:
  * **The Consumer Protection Desk**  
    Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority (UMRA)  
    Plot 39, Nakasero Road, Kampala, Uganda  
    Email: `complaints@umra.go.ug` | Toll Free: `0800 111 444`
