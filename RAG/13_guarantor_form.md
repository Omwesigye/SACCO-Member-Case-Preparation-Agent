## Section 3: Irrevocable Guarantee and Indemnity Agreement
1. **Joint and Several Liability:** In consideration of the SACCO granting a loan facility of **UGX ____________________** to the borrower named herein, I, the undersigned Guarantor, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantee the punctual repayment of the principal, contractual interest, and any associated collection expenses.
2. **Right of Lien and Direct Offset:** I hereby grant the SACCO an immediate, enforceable lien over all my current and future savings, shares, and deposits held with the Society up to the full pledged sum of **UGX ____________________**.
3. **Recovery Authorization:** If the borrower fails to pay any scheduled monthly installment and defaults past sixty (60) days, I explicitly authorize the SACCO Management, without prior court intervention, to:
   * Place an immediate administrative freeze on my savings accounts.
   * Debit my savings balances to offset the borrower's delinquent arrears.
   * Transfer my pledged savings to the loan account until the default is rectified.
4. **Non-Withdrawal Clause:** I understand that I am legally barred from withdrawing my pledged savings or terminating my SACCO membership until this loan is certified as paid in full.
