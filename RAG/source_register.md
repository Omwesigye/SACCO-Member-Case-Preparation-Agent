## Provenance and Citation Governance Rules

1. **Hierarchy of Normative Precedence:**
   $$\text{External Statutory Instruments \& UMRA Guidelines} \succ \text{Registered SACCO Bylaws} \succ \text{Board-Approved Policies} \succ \text{Operational Forms \& Tools}$$
2. **Authoritative Origin Rule:**
   - External statutory acts and UMRA circulars are classified as **External Authoritative Sources**.
   - SACCO policies approved by the Board are classified as **Primary Institutional Sources**.
   - Unapproved drafts, outdated policies, or general internet articles are **strictly rejected** from serving as decision rules for the agent.
3. **Traceable Citations:** All agent responses and case summaries must cite the exact `Source ID`, `Section Heading`, and `Approval Date`.
4. **Separation of Sources:** External legal/regulatory guidance is separated from internal institutional policies and operational templates to avoid conflating internal rules with statutory mandates.
