## 2. Detailed Product Specifications

### 2.1 Development / Business Loan (Code: PR-001)
* **Target Audience:** Established sole proprietors, small retailers, contractors, and commercial farmers.
* **Eligible Purpose:** Working capital expansion, acquisition of commercial inventory, purchase of commercial tools/machinery, construction of rental units.
* **Eligibility Criteria:**
  1. Minimum 6 months continuous SACCO membership.
  2. Fully paid share capital (minimum 20 shares).
  3. Verifiable trading enterprise with an active trading location for at least 12 months.
* **Repayment Frequency:** Monthly installments.
* **Fees:** Appraisal fee 2.0% of loan amount; Loan Insurance Levy 1.0% per annum; Registration fee UGX 20,000.

### 2.2 Agricultural Crop & Livestock Loan (Code: PR-002)
* **Target Audience:** Smallholder and medium-scale farmers engaged in coffee, maize, beans, dairy, or horticulture.
* **Eligible Purpose:** Farm input purchases (improved seeds, fertilizers, veterinary drugs), leasing farmland, farm fencing, irrigation gear.
* **Eligibility Criteria:**
  1. Verified ownership or certified leasehold of agricultural land.
  2. Farming enterprise inspection by SACCO Field Agricultural Officer.
* **Grace Period:** A moratorium on principal of up to 30 days is permitted where the harvest and sale cycle exceeds 90 days. Interest continues to accrue.
* **Fees:** Appraisal fee 1.5% of loan amount; Agricultural field inspection fee UGX 30,000; Insurance levy 1.0%.

### 2.3 School Fees Loan (Code: PR-003)
* **Target Audience:** Parents and guardians with children enrolled in recognized primary, secondary, tertiary, or vocational schools.
* **Eligible Purpose:** Direct payment of tuition, boarding fees, and school requirements.
* **Repayment Frequency:** Bullet or monthly installments aligned to school terms (maximum 4 months).
* **Mandatory Documentation:** Official school circular, fees bank slip/invoice, and parent declaration.
* **Disbursement Option:** The SACCO reserves the right to disburse funds directly to the school bank account via EFT or institutional pay-bill.

### 2.4 Emergency / Medical Loan (Code: PR-004)
* **Target Audience:** Any member experiencing sudden, unforeseen emergencies (illness, bereavement, urgent court bail, disaster relief).
* **Key Feature:** Fast turnaround time (appraisal and disbursement within 4 hours).
* **Security Limitation:** Loan amount cannot exceed 100% of the applicant’s unencumbered savings balance. No external guarantors required.
* **Repayment Tenor:** Strictly 1 to 3 months.

### 2.5 Asset Finance / Bodaboda Loan (Code: PR-005)
* **Target Audience:** Transport operators, delivery riders, artisans, and transport fleet owners.
* **Security & Equity:** Member must contribute a minimum of 25% cash down-payment into their savings account. The asset logbook/title remains registered jointly in the name of the SACCO and the member until 100% repayment.
* **Mandatory Insurance:** Asset must be comprehensively insured with the SACCO noted as first loss payee.

## 3. Product Inclusions and Exclusions
* **Prohibited Purposes:** Speculative securities trading, gambling, illicit goods, refinancing external informal moneylenders without verification, or financing political campaign activities.
* **Concurrent Loans:** A member may hold one core development loan and one short-term emergency or school fees loan simultaneously, provided total debt repayments do not breach the 50% Debt Service Ratio (DSR) limit.
