# Credit Risk Management Policy

## 1. Governance and Risk Philosophy
* **1.1 Objective:** To establish a prudent credit risk management architecture that safeguards depositor funds, minimizes credit defaults, and complies strictly with the Tier 4 Microfinance Institutions and Money Lenders Act, 2016 and UMRA SACCO Regulations 2020.
* **1.2 Role of the Risk Committee:** The Board Risk and Audit Committee oversees credit risk tolerances, evaluates Portfolio at Risk (PAR), reviews quarterly loan aging schedules, and recommends loan loss provisioning levels.

## 2. Risk Appetite and Concentration Limits
* **2.1 Maximum Portfolio at Risk (PAR):**
  * Portfolio at Risk $> 30$ days ($\text{PAR}_{30}$) shall not exceed **5.0%** of total outstanding gross loan portfolio at any time.
  * Corrective operational actions are immediately triggered if $\text{PAR}_{30}$ reaches 3.5%.
* **2.2 Single Borrower Concentration Limit:**
  * No individual borrower may hold total credit facilities exceeding **5.0% of the SACCO's Core Capital**.
  * No group or connected borrowership may exceed **10.0% of Core Capital**.
* **2.3 Sectoral Concentration Limits:**
  * Agriculture / Crop / Livestock: Maximum 40% of total loan portfolio.
  * Trade / Commercial / Small Retail: Maximum 45% of total loan portfolio.
  * Bodaboda / Transport Asset Finance: Maximum 15% of total loan portfolio.
* **2.4 Insider Lending Ceiling:**
  * Aggregate lending to all Directors, Committee Members, and staff shall **never exceed 25% of Core Capital**.
  * Every insider facility must be 100% secured with liquid unencumbered cash savings or registered real estate titles.

## 3. Mandatory Loan Classification and Provisioning Schedule
In accordance with Regulation 18 and Schedule 2 of the Tier 4 SACCO Regulations 2020, all loan facilities must be continuously classified and provisioned according to their aging status:

| Classification Category | Days Past Due (Arrears) | Qualitative Criteria | Mandatory Specific Provision (%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Performing (Pass / Normal)** | 0 to 30 days | Repayments made on time; borrower financially stable. | **1%** (General portfolio provision) |
| **2. Watch (Special Mention)** | 31 to 60 days | Potential weakness identified; late installment; temporary cash flow hitch. | **5%** |
| **3. Substandard** | 61 to 90 days | Inadequate repayment capacity; primary source of repayment compromised. | **20%** |
| **4. Doubtful** | 91 to 180 days | Full collection is highly improbable; substantial loss expected. | **50%** |
| **5. Loss** | > 180 days | Uncollectible; little or no recovery potential; security deficit. | **100%** |

## 4. Automatic Delinquency Controls and Loan Gating
* **4.1 Applicant Gating:** A member with any active facility in Category 2 (Watch), 3 (Substandard), 4 (Doubtful), or 5 (Loss) is automatically disqualified from receiving new credit or acting as a guarantor.
* **4.2 Guarantor Exposure Freeze:** If a borrower enters Category 3 (Substandard, $>60$ days), an immediate freeze is placed on all pledged guarantor savings across the SACCO core banking system.
* **4.3 Credit Bureau Inquiry:** All loan applications exceeding UGX 5,000,000 must undergo a mandatory Credit Reference Bureau (CRB) inquiry to detect external delinquency across commercial banks and microfinance institutions.

## 5. Restructuring and Rescheduling Protocols
* **5.1 Eligibility for Restructuring:** A loan may only be rescheduled if the borrower has demonstrated goodwill, experienced an involuntary and verifiable adverse event (e.g., severe crop disease, flood, documented hospitalization), and has paid at least 20% of the original principal.
* **5.2 Prohibition of Concealment:** Rescheduling shall never be used to conceal delinquency or avoid regulatory provisioning. Rescheduled facilities remain classified in their prior risk tier for at least three (3) consecutive successful monthly payments before upgrading.
* **5.3 Approval Level:** All loan restructurings require explicit prior approval by the Credit Committee and formal notation to the full Board of Directors.
