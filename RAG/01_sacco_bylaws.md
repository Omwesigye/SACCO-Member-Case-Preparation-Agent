# Standard SACCO Registered Bylaws

## Section 1: Name, Area of Operation, and Registered Office
* **Clause 1.1:** The Society shall be registered and known as **Group X  Cooperative Savings and Credit Society Limited** 
* **Clause 1.2:** The registered office and principal operating address of the Society shall be at Plot 14, Main Street, P.O. Box 112, Kampala District, Uganda.
* **Clause 1.3:** The area of operation of the Society shall be within the central Region and surrounding districts of the Republic of Uganda, subject to the licensing and approval of the Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority (UMRA).

## Section 2: Objectives and Principles of the Society
* **Clause 2.1:** The primary objective of the SACCO is to promote thrift, mobilize savings, and create a source of affordable and sustainable credit for members for provident and productive purposes.
* **Clause 2.2:** The SACCO shall operate in accordance with international cooperative values (self-help, self-responsibility, democracy, equality, equity, and solidarity) and the Tier 4 Microfinance Institutions and Money Lenders Act, 2016.

## Section 3: Membership Eligibility and Admission Procedures
* **Clause 3.1: Common Bond Qualification:** Any individual of Ugandan nationality, aged 18 years and above, of good character and sound mind, who resides, works, or carries on economic activity within the defined area of operation, is eligible for membership.
* **Clause 3.2: Application and Entrance Fee:** Application for membership shall be submitted in writing on the prescribed form accompanied by:
  1. A non-refundable entrance/registration fee of UGX 25,000.
  2. Proof of identity (valid Ugandan National ID / NIN).
  3. A verified Local Council 1 (LC1) recommendation letter.
* **Clause 3.3: Minimum Share Capital:** Every member must subscribe and fully pay for a minimum of twenty (20) ordinary shares at par value of UGX 10,000 each (Total Minimum Share Capital: UGX 200,000). A member shall not hold more than 20% of the total issued share capital of the SACCO at any time.
* **Clause 3.4: Probation Period:** An admitted member must save continuously for a minimum period of six (6) consecutive months before becoming eligible to apply for credit from the SACCO.

## Section 4: Cessation and Termination of Membership
* **Clause 4.1: Voluntary Withdrawal:** A member may withdraw from the Society by giving sixty (60) days written notice to the Board of Directors, provided the member has no outstanding loan balance and is not acting as a guarantor for an active or defaulted loan.
* **Clause 4.2: Settlement upon Withdrawal:** Upon expiry of notice, the member’s non-pledged savings and share capital (transferred or refunded per cooperative law) shall be disbursed after offsetting any legitimate claims.

## Section 5: Capital Structure and Financial Resources
* **Clause 5.1:** The capital of the SACCO shall consist of:
  1. Unlimited number of ordinary shares of par value UGX 10,000 each.
  2. Statutory Reserve Fund into which at least 20% of the net operational surplus must be transferred annually.
  3. Voluntary and compulsory savings mobilized from members.
  4. Retained earnings, grants, and external borrowings approved by the AGM within UMRA statutory limits.

## Section 6: Organs of Governance and Delegation
* **Clause 6.1: Annual General Meeting (AGM):** The supreme decision-making authority of the Society is vested in the General Meeting of members.
* **Clause 6.2: Board of Directors:** Consists of nine (9) members elected by the AGM for a term of three (3) years, renewable once. The Board exercises general governance, policy formulation, and fiduciary oversight.
* **Clause 6.3: Credit Committee:** Consists of three (3) members appointed by the Board from among their number, responsible for reviewing loan appraisals, approving credit within delegated thresholds, and enforcing credit policy compliance.
* **Clause 6.4: Supervisory Committee:** Consists of three (3) independent members elected by the AGM to function as an internal audit mechanism reporting directly to the members.

## Section 7: Loans to Members
* **Clause 7.1:** Loans shall only be made to bona fide, active members who have completed their probation period, met the minimum shareholding, and saved continuously.
* **Clause 7.2: Loan Multiplier Limit:** No member shall be granted a total credit exposure exceeding four (4) times their unencumbered accumulated savings, subject to affordability and collateral requirements.
* **Clause 7.3: Insider Lending:** Any loan to a Director, Committee Member, or staff member must receive unanimous approval of the full Board, be declared at the next AGM, and comply strictly with Regulation 12 of the Tier 4 SACCO Regulations 2020.

## Section 8: Dispute Resolution and Regulatory Recourse
* **Clause 8.1:** Any dispute arising out of the interpretation of these Bylaws or Society business that cannot be resolved amicably shall be referred to arbitration under the Cooperative Societies Act or submitted to UMRA for regulatory mediation.
