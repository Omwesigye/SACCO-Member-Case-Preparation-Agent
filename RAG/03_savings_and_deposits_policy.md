# Savings and Deposits Policy

## 1. Purpose and Regulatory Framework
* **1.1 Objective:** To establish clear guidelines for deposit mobilization, classification, liquidity management, and member withdrawal rights in compliance with the Tier 4 Microfinance Institutions and Money Lenders Act, 2016 and UMRA SACCO Regulations 2020.
* **1.2 Scope:** Applies to all registered members maintaining deposit and savings accounts with the SACCO.

## 2. Classification of Savings and Deposit Products
The SACCO operates three primary deposit accounts:
* **2.1 Compulsory Ordinary Savings (Prime Shares/Savings):**
  * Mandatory monthly contribution required from all active members (Minimum UGX 20,000 per month).
  * Serves as the primary basis for calculating credit borrowing eligibility and collateral multipliers.
* **2.2 Voluntary / Demand Savings Account:**
  * Discretionary savings that may be deposited and withdrawn at will, subject to normal banking hours and minimum ledger balance rules.
  * Earns annual interest if balance exceeds UGX 500,000 continuously.
* **2.3 Fixed / Term Deposit Account:**
  * Fixed-tenor contractual deposits for 3, 6, 12, or 24 months.
  * Minimum deposit: UGX 1,000,000.
  * Premature termination incurs a 50% penalty on accrued interest.

## 3. Minimum Account Balances and Maintenance
* **3.1 Minimum Operating Balance:** Every active member account must maintain an untouchable minimum ledger balance of **UGX 20,000**.
* **3.2 Inactive and Dormant Accounts:**
  * An account with no deposit or withdrawal activity for six (6) consecutive months is classified as **Inactive**.
  * An account inactive for twelve (12) consecutive months is classified as **Dormant**. Re-activation requires physical appearance, formal written request, and KYC re-verification.

## 4. Pledged vs. Free (Unencumbered) Savings
* **4.1 Collateral Lien:** When a member takes a loan or signs as a guarantor for another member’s loan, an automated legal lien is placed on the member's Compulsory and Voluntary savings equal to the pledged amount.
* **4.2 Definition of Free Savings:**
  $$\text{Free Savings} = \text{Total Member Savings} - (\text{Outstanding Own Loan Balances} + \text{Total Active Pledged Guarantorships})$$
* **4.3 Restrictions on Pledged Savings:** Under no circumstances shall a member withdraw, transfer, or offset savings that are currently pledged as security for an active loan or guarantee, unless substituted with equivalent approved collateral.

## 5. Withdrawal Rules and Notice Periods
* **5.1 Over-the-Counter Daily Limit:** Daily cash withdrawal limit is UGX 2,000,000 per member without prior notice.
* **5.2 Prior Notice for Large Withdrawals:**
  * Withdrawals between UGX 2,000,001 and UGX 10,000,000 require twenty-four (24) hours written notice.
  * Withdrawals exceeding UGX 10,000,000 require seven (7) days written notice.
* **5.3 Compulsory Savings Withdrawal:** Compulsory savings cannot be withdrawn during the currency of SACCO membership; they are refundable only upon formal cessation of membership with sixty (60) days notice.

## 6. Interest on Savings Deposits
* **6.1 Compulsory Savings:** Earns an annual dividend/interest declared at the AGM based on annual net operational surplus (historically 6% to 8% per annum).
* **6.2 Fixed Deposits:** Rates range from 8% to 12% per annum depending on term and deposit volume, as specified in the [Board-Approved Interest Rate and Fee Schedule](file:///d:/Sacco-Agent/RAG/20_board_approved_interest_rate_and_fee_schedule.md).
* **6.3 Withholding Tax:** In accordance with Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) statutory tax laws, withholding tax (15%) is deducted at source from gross interest earnings where applicable.
