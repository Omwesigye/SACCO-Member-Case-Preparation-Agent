# Governance Charter and Committee Terms of Reference

## 1. Governance Architecture and Legal Basis
* **1.1 Statutory Authority:** Established under the Cooperative Societies Act Cap 112, the Tier 4 Microfinance Institutions and Money Lenders Act, 2016, and the registered SACCO Bylaws.
* **1.2 Governance Hierarchy:**
  $$\text{Annual General Meeting (AGM)} \longrightarrow \text{Board of Directors} \longrightarrow \text{Committees (Credit, Supervisory)} \longrightarrow \text{CEO / Management}$$

## 2. Board of Directors Mandate and Fit-and-Proper Criteria
* **2.1 Composition:** Nine (9) non-executive directors elected by the AGM.
* **2.2 Fit-and-Proper Requirements:** Pursuant to UMRA regulatory guidance:
  * Must hold a minimum of Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) or equivalent.
  * Must possess a clean financial and credit record (no personal default in any financial institution).
  * Must not have been convicted of any offence involving dishonesty, fraud, or breach of trust.
* **2.3 Fiduciary Responsibilities:** Formulation of strategic plans, approval of operational policies, prudential risk limits, and oversight of statutory UMRA reporting.

## 3. Terms of Reference: Credit Committee
* **3.1 Composition:** Three (3) directors appointed by the Board, with the Head of Credit acting as an ex-officio secretary (non-voting).
* **3.2 Quorum and Meeting Frequency:**
  * Quorum requires a minimum of two (2) voting members.
  * Meets weekly or on-demand to review submitted loan case packs.
* **3.3 Mandate and Decision Powers:**
  1. Review verified loan appraisal case packs submitted by Branch Credit Officers.
  2. Approve, decline, or defer loan requests between **UGX 3,000,001 and UGX 20,000,000**.
  3. Ensure all approved loans strictly comply with the [Credit Policy](file:///d:/Sacco-Agent/RAG/02_credit_policy_and_lending_procedures_manual.md) and UMRA exposure limits.
  4. Escalate applications exceeding UGX 20,000,000 or any policy exception requests to the Full Board.
* **3.4 Mandatory Decision Outcomes:**
  * **Approve:** File satisfies all criteria; conditions (if any) clearly minuted.
  * **Decline:** File fails core eligibility or affordability; specific policy grounds must be recorded in writing.
  * **Defer / Incomplete:** Crucial documentation or verification missing; returned to Loan Officer with an explicit list of defects.

## 4. Terms of Reference: Supervisory Committee
* **4.1 Composition:** Three (3) members elected directly by the AGM, operating independently of the Board of Directors.
* **4.2 Mandate:**
  1. Conduct continuous internal audits of credit appraisals, cash management, and ledger entries.
  2. Inspect loan files post-disbursement to confirm that collateral and guarantor documents exist and are valid.
  3. Report quarterly directly to the members at the AGM and submit regulatory inspection copies to UMRA.

## 5. Conflict of Interest and Mandatory Recusal Protocol
* **5.1 Recusal Duty:** Any member of the Credit Committee, Board, or credit staff who has a personal, familial, or business connection with an applicant or guarantor must:
  1. Formally declare the conflict of interest before file deliberations commence.
  2. Physically withdraw from the meeting room during appraisal, discussion, and voting on the file.
  3. Refrain from signing any approval minute or disbursement authorization for that file.
* **5.2 Breach Penalties:** Failure to disclose a conflict of interest constitutes gross misconduct, resulting in immediate suspension from committee duties and notification to UMRA.
