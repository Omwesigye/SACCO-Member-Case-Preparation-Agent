## 5. Delinquency Penalties and Default Charges
* **Default Penalty Rate:** **2.0% per month** charged strictly on the **overdue principal portion** for each month the installment remains unpaid.
* **Prohibition of Usurious Compounding:** In line with UMRA guidance:
  * Penalties shall **never be levied on accrued interest** or previously accumulated penalties.
  * The cumulative aggregate of penalty charges shall not exceed twenty-five percent (25%) of the total original loan principal.
* **Grace Period on Penalties:** A grace period of five (5) days following the scheduled monthly due date is granted before late payment penalties are applied to the member's account.
