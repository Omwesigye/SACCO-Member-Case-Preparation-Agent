POLICY ID: SAV-001
TITLE: Member Savings Policy

1. SAVINGS INFORMATION

Savings information used during loan case preparation
must come from an approved member record.

RULE ID: SAV-001-RECORD


2. SAVINGS BALANCE

A member's savings balance may be considered when preparing
a loan case.

However, the savings balance alone must never be interpreted
as automatic qualification for a loan.

RULE ID: SAV-001-BALANCE


3. MONTHLY SAVINGS

The member's regular monthly savings may be included in the
financial information presented to the human reviewer.

RULE ID: SAV-001-MONTHLY


4. DECISION AUTHORITY

Savings information may support case preparation but the
final financial decision remains with authorized SACCO staff.

RULE ID: SAV-001-AUTHORITY
