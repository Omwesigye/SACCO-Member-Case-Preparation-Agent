POLICY ID: LOAN-001
TITLE: Development Loan Policy

1. MEMBERSHIP REQUIREMENT

A member applying for a Development Loan must have maintained
active SACCO membership for at least six months.

RULE ID: LOAN-001-MEMBERSHIP


2. KYC REQUIREMENT

A member applying for a loan must have completed the SACCO's
Know Your Customer verification requirements.

RULE ID: LOAN-001-KYC


3. ARREARS

A member with outstanding loan arrears requires additional
human review before another loan application can proceed.

RULE ID: LOAN-001-ARREARS


4. REPAYMENT TERM

Development Loans may have a repayment period between
6 months and 24 months.

RULE ID: LOAN-001-TERM


5. GUARANTORS

Development Loan applications require guarantor information.

The appropriate number and suitability of guarantors must
be confirmed by an authorized SACCO officer.

RULE ID: LOAN-001-GUARANTOR


6. LOAN APPROVAL

Final loan approval or rejection may only be performed
by authorized SACCO personnel or the Credit Committee.

An automated system or AI assistant must not approve,
reject, or disburse a loan.

RULE ID: LOAN-001-APPROVAL
