POLICY ID: REP-001
TITLE: Illustrative Repayment Policy

1. ILLUSTRATIVE SCHEDULE

Repayment schedules generated during case preparation
are illustrative only.

RULE ID: REP-001-ILLUSTRATIVE


2. CALCULATION

Repayment calculations must be generated by an approved
deterministic calculator and not invented by an AI model.

RULE ID: REP-001-CALCULATOR


3. LOAN OFFER

An illustrative repayment schedule does not constitute
a loan offer, loan approval, or final repayment agreement.

RULE ID: REP-001-OFFER


4. FINAL TERMS

Final loan terms are established only after the appropriate
human review and authorization.

RULE ID: REP-001-AUTHORIZATION
